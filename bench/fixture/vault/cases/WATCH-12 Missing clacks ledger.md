# WATCH-12 Missing clacks ledger

The ledger of the Grand Trunk tower on the Tump went missing on Octeday night. Reported by
Adora Belle Dearheart. Assigned to [[Carrot Ironfoundersson]] and [[Angua]].

## Facts

- The tower log shows the ledger signed out at two bells by a clerk nobody can name.
- Angua found the scent of printer's ink on the shelf. That points at the Guild of Engravers.
- [[Nobby Nobbs|Nobby]] saw a cart leave Tump Lane before dawn.

## Decision

We will not search the Guild of Engravers without a warrant.
Because Vetinari wants the guilds kept calm until the audit is done, a search without one costs more than it finds.
