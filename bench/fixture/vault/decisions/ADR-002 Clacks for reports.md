# ADR-002 Clacks for reports

## Context

Reports from the outer Watch Houses reached Pseudopolis Yard a day late by runner.

## Decision

Every Watch House sends its shift report over the clacks at the end of the shift.

## Why

A report by runner is a day old when it arrives, and the clacks takes minutes.
The Grand Trunk gave the Watch a fixed price, so the cost is known. Codes are in [[Clacks codes]].
