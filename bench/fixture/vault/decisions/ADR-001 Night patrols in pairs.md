# ADR-001 Night patrols in pairs

## Context

Two constables were hurt in the Shades in one week, each walking alone.

## Decision

Night patrols in the Shades go in pairs, and no officer walks Elm Street alone after dark.

## Why

A lone officer in the Shades is a target; a pair gets home.
It costs four more officers on the night [[Rota]], which [[Sam Vimes|Vimes]] accepted.

## Consequences

The day shift at Cable Street loses two officers until the new recruits are sworn in.
